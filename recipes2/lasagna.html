Lasagna Recipe


A delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

One of the most popular dishes in Italy!

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the cooked beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce, followed by a layer of noodles, then a layer of ricotta cheese. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce on top.
  6. Sprinkle mozzarella and Parmesan cheese over the top.
  7. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes, or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  8. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
